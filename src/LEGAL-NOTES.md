# Product-identity guardrails

Openstrator should remain an original vector editor rather than a branded clone.

Recommended guardrails:
- Do not use Adobe, Illustrator, Ai, Creative Cloud, or Adobe-owned marks in the app name, icon, store metadata, splash screen, onboarding, or marketing except where nominative reference is legally appropriate.
- Do not copy Adobe artwork, icons, panel illustrations, exact splash screens, proprietary fonts, sample assets, or source code.
- Avoid pixel-for-pixel replication of another product's overall visual identity. Familiar editing concepts can be implemented with an original macOS-native layout and icon language.
- Implement features from public behavior/specifications and your own code rather than copying protected implementation material.
- Keep third-party file-format support documented and test interoperability independently.
- Before commercial release, obtain qualified legal review for trademarks, trade dress, licenses, bundled fonts/assets, and any patented functionality relevant to your markets.

This file is product-design guidance, not legal advice and not a guarantee against legal claims.
